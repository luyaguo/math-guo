第六部分 形容词和副词

一、形容词

形容词主要用于描写或修饰名词（或代词），在句子中可以作定语、表语、补语成分。

1、形容词的位置

英语中形容词作定语成分时，有后置和前置两种情况，即位于被修饰词之前或之后，以下举例几种常见形式。

1-1 修饰名词时前置（最常见）

许多形容词皆可置于它所修饰的名词之前。这一情况最为常见。

New, disruptive technology sometimes demands novel applications of the Constitution’s protections.

  • 译文：新的颠覆性技术有时候要求对宪法的保护采用新颖的运用。
  • 该句中 new 与 disruptive 两个形容词位于名词 technology 前，对其进行修饰，表示“新的颠覆性技术”。
  • novel 这一形容词位于名词 applications 前对其进行修饰，表示“新颖的运用”。

Good governance rests on an understanding of the inherent worth of each individual.

  • 译文：良好的管理有赖于对每个个体内在价值的理解。
  • 该句中形容词 good 位于名词 governance 前对其进行修饰，表示“良好的管理”；
  • inherent 这一形容词位于名词 worth 前对其进行修饰，表示“内在的价值”。

1-2 修饰复合不定代词时需要后置

当形容词作定语修饰由 some、any、every、no 等和别的词构成的复合不定代词时须后置。

常见的复合不定代词如：something、someone、somewhere、anything、anyone、everything 等。

Some important thing 此处修饰词应离 thing 更近，故该改写为 something important

限定词 修饰词 名词

Now something similar could be happening in the oceans.【2006，T2】

译文：现在，类似的事情可能正在海洋中发生。

A “southern” camp headed by France wants something different.【2011（二）T4】

译文：以法国为首的“南部”阵营却持不同想法。

Anyone intelligent can do it.

译文：任何有头脑的人都能做这件事。

1-3 一些特殊的形容词位置

(1) enough 作形容词时，作定语，可位于修饰的名词前或后。

I have enough time.（前置）

I have time enough. (后置)

译文：我有足够的时间

(2) past, positive, total, following 等作形容词时，可以置于修饰词的名词之前或之后。

the following days = the days following. 以后的日子

total sum = sum total 总数

(3) 表语形容词用作定语时需要后置

有这种使用情况的表语形容词有：alive（活着的），ablaze（着火的、发亮的），afloat（漂浮的）等， 一般常作表语成分，用于描述主语的性质、状态等。

He was probably the most famous film critic alive.

译文：他可能是在世的最著名的影评人。

The boats afloat were not seen by the enemy.

译文：水上的小船没有被敌人发现。

2、形容词的顺序【重要】

More recently, while examining housing construction, the researchers discovered that illiterate, non-English-speaking Mexican workers in Houston, Texas, consistently met best-practice labor productivity standards despite the complexity of the building industry’s work.

最近，在研究住房建设时，研究人员发现,尽管建筑行业的工作复杂，但德克萨斯州休斯顿的文盲、不 会说英语的墨西哥工人，始终符合最佳实践劳动生产率标准。

相对性排序

相对性排序：形容词之间相互比较，

越是相对不变（客观、属性、种类、特质）的越靠近名词！

越是相对易变（主观、态度、情感、可有可无）的越远离名词！

  • an expensive new Japanese sports car. 一辆新的昂贵的日本跑车
  • all those twenty beautiful small yellow old French wood table. 那二十张漂亮的黄色法国小木桌

练习

1）I like a coat. (woollen / red /beautiful / Chinese / new) 我喜欢一件外套。（羊毛的/红色的/漂亮的/中式的/新的）

  • I like a beautiful new red Chinese woollen coat.

2）He graduated from a school. (prestigious / American / law/ old) 他毕业于一所学校。（有声望的/美国的/法律的/旧的）

  • He graduated from a prestigious old American law school.

3）There is a pen. (plastic / black / new / British / nice) 有一支钢笔。（塑料的/黑色的/新的/英国的/漂亮的）

  • There is a nice new black British plastic pen.

3、复合形容词【重要】

复合形容词就是两个或两个以上的词加连字符形成的形容词，通常置于名词前。

名词词组的形式：限定词 + 修饰词（形容词性）+ 名词

中间的修饰语不能放短语，要么中间放一个单词，要么放一个复合形容词（A-B-C）

以下为一些常用的构词形式，需注意，我们在使用复合形容词时，尽量以模仿、积累为主，避免自行组合“编造”，否则可能会造成歧义与错误。

1）数词+名词

A program will last five years.

→ This is a five-year program.

years 要改为 year，因为此处的 five-year 做修饰词用，相当于形容词，形容词没有单复数。

2）数词+名词+形容词

John is five years old.

→ A five-year-old boy.

时间（地点）单位的名词可以当表时间（地点）副词使用。

如：next weekend/ last year/ 3 miles

3）名词+现在分词

Mary is a girl who likes to break boy’s hearts.

→ Mary is a heart-breaking girl.

此处 breaking 为现在分词状态，表示 Marry 伤别人的心，若改为过去分词状态，意思则会改变为 Marry 被别人伤心。

从右往左看：Mary is a heart-breaking girl. ←

4）名词+过去分词

The boy’s heart is broken.

→ the heart-broken boy

This was a war-tom country.（饱受战争摧残 tear （tearing/ tom） war）

Mary’s miniskirt is indeed eye-catching in the picture.（注意的 eye/ catch）

主 系 表 【表语修饰主语，主语可以放到表语后面来看。】

5）副词+现在分词

The area which lay low was flooded.

→ The low-lying area was flooded.

lie v.躺；位于；说谎；n.谎言；位置；

过去分词：lain；过去式：lay；现在分词：lying；第三人称单数：lies

此处用 doing 形式是因为此处为主动进行态，done 为被动完成态

6）well 或 ill + 过去分词

  • well-known 很岀名的
  • well-dressed 穿着很体面的
  • ill-considered 考不周的
  • ill-judged 判断不当的

7）形容词性的词+名词变成的过去分词（即名词动词化）

a girl who has big eyes

→ a big-eyed girl

8）直接连接

The problem is hard to solve.

→ That hard-to-solve problem

二、副词

副词是指在句子中表示行为或状态特征的词，用以修饰 动词、形容词、其他副词或全句，表示时间、地点、程度、方式等概念。副词在意义和形式上有自己的特征。

1、副词的常见分类

(1) 时间副词

常见的有 now, then, soon, ago, recently, lately, later, before, early, today, tomorrow, yesterday, tonight, suddenly, immediately, still, already, just 等。

例：They are noting that the Victorians suddenly seem to become more human.【2021 (一) T3】
译文：他们注意到，维多利亚时代的人似乎变得更有人情味了。

(2) 地点副词

常见的有 here, there, up, down, away, nearby, home, ahead, abroad, indoors, overseas, upstairs, downstairs 等。

例：The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events in the UK and more abroad.【2017（二）T1】
译文：公园跑一开始由十几个朋友发起，后来在英国引发了400场活动，在国外就更多了。

(3) 方式副词

表示行为方式的副词大多以 -ly 结尾，常见的有 well, fast, hard, quietly, heavily, warmly, carefully, happily, politely, angrily 等。

例：These habits have helped companies earn billions of dollars when customers eat snacks or wipe counters almost without thinking, often in response to a carefully designed set of daily cues.【2010（二）T3】
译文：消费者通常受一系列精心设计的日常暗示的影响，他们几乎不假思索地吃着零食或者擦拭柜台，这些行为习惯已经帮助各公司赚取了数十亿美元的收益。

(4) 频度副词

常见的有 always, usually, often, frequently, constantly, occasionally, sometimes, seldom, hardly, rarely, never 等。

例：Those who jumped without a job haven’t always landed in top positions quickly.【2011（二）T2】
译文：那些没有找好工作就跳槽的人并不总是很快就能找到最高层的职位。

(5) 程度副词

常见的有 fairly, pretty, rather, quite, very, much, too, greatly, almost, nearly, half, highly, deeply, partly, perfectly, really 等。

例：For the rest of the decade she apparently managed both roles without attracting much criticism.【2011（二）T1】
译文：在此后九年时间里，她貌似成功地应付了两个角色，又没有招致太多非议。

(6) 连接副词

这类副词有相当于并列连词的 therefore, besides, otherwise, however, moreover, furthermore, still, thus, meanwhile 等，也有引导名词性从句或不定式的 when, why, where, how 等。

As the industry advances, however, other suits may have an even greater impact.【2012（二）T3】

  • 译文：但是，随着该行业的发展，其他案例可能会有更大的影响。
  • ==but 是连词，可以用 but 连接两个句子，但 however 是副词，不能直接替换 but，要改成; however。==
  • ==本句中 as 使前句丧失独立性，所以可以用, however, 连接==

(7) 关系副词

就是引导定语从句的 when, where, why 等。

“We are just in a situation where there are no good theories explaining what consciousness actually is and how you could ever build a machine to get there.”
译文：我们正处于这样一种境地，即没有合理的理论可以解释意识到底是什么，以及如何创造一台拥有人类意识的机器。”

(8) 疑问副词

就是用于引出特殊疑问句的 when, where, why, how 等。

例：When will he be back?

译文：他什么时候回来？

(9) 评论性副词

就是修饰整个句子，表达说话人的观点态度的副词，常见的有 frankly, honestly, obviously, evidently, generally, briefly, fortunately, unfortunately, luckily, naturally, hopefully 等。

例：Unfortunately, the polyurethane foam he used is inherently unstable.【2022（一）T1】
译文：不幸的是，他使用的聚氨酯泡沫本身就不稳定。

2、副词的位置

副词的位置取决于副词的类型和它所修饰的成分。

The boss wanted this man to be punished badly. 【不佳】
→ The boss badly wanted this man to be punished.
→ The boss wanted this man to be badly punished.

译文：老板要这个人受到严厉处罚。

副词在句中常见位置

副词类型 位置
修饰形容词或副词 一般放在被修饰词之前（如：very young）
时间副词 可以放于句首，也可以放于句尾，有时还可以放于动词前面（如： tomorrow morning I’ll go to the park）
地点副词 一般说来，地点副词经常置于句末，往往紧跟动词(+宾语)后（如：The little boy rushed downstairs）
方式副词 主要放在动词(+宾语)的后面，在表示特别强调时，偶尔也可放在实义动词的前面，第一个助动词的后面（如：She likes to do things properly）
程度副词 修饰形容词和副词时，应放在形容词和副词前面，在修饰动词时，常常放在第一个助词后，实义动词之前（如：You are such a likeable person. I greatly enjoy working with you）
频度副词 可以置于句首，也可以置于句末，还可以置于第一个助动词后（如： Sometimes our fate resembles a fruit tree in winter）
评论性副词 常置于句首，其后常有逗号与主句隔开；有时置于句末，其前常有逗号与主句隔开（如：Unfortunately, the show was one of the dullest we have ever seen）
疑问副词、连接副词、关系副词以及修饰整个句子的副词 通常放在句子或从句的前面（如：When will you be ready?）
enough（作为副词） 总是置于被修饰的形容词或副词之后（如：old enough）

三、比较级和最高级

1、比较级和最高级的形成规则

英语中，比较级和最高级是形容词和副词的重要语法形式，用于表示不同程度的比较。比较级主要用于两者之间的比较，最高级用于三者及三者以上之间的比较。

比较级和最高级的形成规则主要取决于形容词或副词的音节数。具体规则如下：

比较级和最高级的形成规则

形容词、副词的比较级和最高级的形成规则单音节词比较级通常在词尾加“-er”；最高级通常在词尾加“-est”例：tall→taller→tallest fast→faster→fastest great→greater→greatest
双音节词看尾巴（观察词的后缀）：若形容词、副词以 -ous/-ive/-ic/-al/-ly结尾，变化比较级/最高级时在该词前加 more/most；没有上述这些后缀的词可以加 -er/est 来变化比较级和最高级，也可以在词之前添加more/most.例：active→more active→most active basic→more basic→most basic clever→cleverer/more clever → cleverest/most clever clear→clearer/more clear → clearest/most clear
多音节词比较级一般在该词前添加“more”；最高级一般在该词前添加“most”。例：interesting→more interesting → most interesting expensive→more expensive → most expensive important → more important → most important
特殊情况一些形容词和副词的比较级/最高级变化是不规则的，可以单独记忆。例：good/well → better→best bad/badly→worse→worst far → farther/further→farthest/furthest

2、比较级和最高级

以两个简单句为例，将两句连接成一个含有比较级的主从复合句，分别说明大于、等于、小于的常用表述方法。

步骤：

  1. 写出两个简单句。

  2. 找出两个简单句中的比较点。【第一：adj/ adv; 第二：相同】

  3. 用以下词组把两个简单句连接成一个主从复合句。

  4. 省略比较状语从句中的相同的比较点。

  5. 美化：省略、倒装、换代词。

2.1 比较级 大于

more…than 用于表示“大于”

（1）形容词

His hair is long. 他的头发很长。

My hair is long. 我的头发很长。 【比较点：long】

→ His hair is more long than my hair is long .

→ His hair is longer than my hair is long .

→ His hair is longer than my hair is .【省略相同比较点 long】

→ His hair is longer than is my hair. 【倒装】

→ His hair is longer than my hair.【省略 is – 不产生歧义】

→ His hair is longer than mine.【代名词】

译文：他的头发比我的头发长。

比较级和最高级变换规则：

(1) 单音节：-er; -est ;

(2) 多音节：more; most;

(3) 双音节：看尾巴：有-ous/-ive/-ic/-al/ly，用 more/most；没有这些尾巴的都可

简单记忆：二分法-- 【 A or 非A 】

**单音节用 er/ est，其他的都用 more/ most。**His hair is longer than my hair is.

练习：将以下两组简单句组合成为含比较级的主从复合句。

(1) His hair is long. My hair is long.
(2) Xiao is old. Cui is old.

（2）副词

He swims well. 他游泳游得很好。

I swim well. 我游泳游得很好。 【比较点：well】

→ He swims more well than I swim well.

→ He swims better than I swim.

→ He swims better than I do. 【省略】

→ He swims better than I. 【省略 do，可能会有歧义，比的是我和他还是游泳这件事？】

→ He swims better than do I. 【倒装】

译文：他游泳比我游的好。

2.2 比较级 等于

as…as 常用于表示"等于”，意为…和…一样…

（1）形容词

My feet are big. 我的脚很大。

Your feet are big. 你的脚很大。
→ My feet are as big as your feet are big.
→ My feet are as big as your feet are.
→ My feet are as big as your feet.
→ My feet are as big as are your feet.
→ My feet are as big as yours.
我的脚和你的一样大。

（2）副词

Bob speaks English fast. 鲍勃英语说得很快。

Lucy speaks English fast. 露西英语说得很快。
→ Bob speaks English as fast as Lucy speaks English fast.
→ Bob speaks English as fast as Lucy speaks English.
→ Bob speaks English as fast as Lucy speaks.
→ Bob speaks English as fast as Lucy does.
→ Bob speaks English as fast as Lucy.
→ Bob speaks English as fast as does Lucy.

2.3 比较级 小于

less … than 少于 (小于)
not so … as 不如…那么… (小于)
not as … as 不如…那么… (小于)

程度副词 从属化连词

（1）形容词

My computer is expensive. 我的电脑很贵。

Your computer is expensive. 你的电脑很贵。

→ My computer is less expensive than your computer is expensive.
→ My computer is less expensive than your computer is expensive.
→ My computer is less expensive than your computer is.
→ My computer is less expensive than your computer.
→ My computer is less expensive than is your computer.
→ My computer is less expensive than yours.
我的电脑没有你的电脑贵。

（2）副词

Tom works hard. 汤姆工作很努力。

Jack works hard. 杰克工作很努力。

→ Tom does not work as hard as Jack works hard. 【比较状语从句】
→ Tom does not work as hard as Jack works hard.
→ Tom does not work as hard as Jack works.
→ Tom does not work as hard as Jack does.
→ Tom does not work as hard as Jack.
→ Tom does not work as hard as does Jack.
汤姆工作没有杰克那么多努力。

2.4 it/ one/ that/ those 【难点】

1、Mountains in Beijing are high. 北京的山很高。

2、Mountains in Tibet are high. 西藏的山很高。
比较：北京的山没有西藏的山高。
→ Mountains in Beijing are not as high as mountains in Tibet are high.
→ Mountains in Beijing are not as high as mountains in Tibet are high.
→ Mountains in Beijing are not as high as are mountains in Tibet.【倒装】
→ Mountains in Beijing are not as high as mountains in Tibet.【省略，有重复的内容 mountains】
→ Mountains in Beijing are not as high as those in Tibet.

a/ an——one

the——this/ these；that/ those

it 用法

it 特指之前提到的同一个事物（特指同物，可数名词单数）。表复数时：用 they / them

This pen is mine. You can use it.

这支笔是我的。你可以使用它。（同一只笔）
These pens are mine. You can use them.

这些笔是我的。你可以使用它们。

one 用法

one：泛指之前提到的同类中的任一个人/物（泛指同类不同物，可数名词单数）。表复数时：用 ones。

I’ve lost my umbrella. I need to buy one.

我的伞丢了．我需要买一个．（不是同一把伞）

I have a new pen and some old ones.

我有一支新笔和一些旧笔。

this、that 用法

this、that：特指之前提到的同类中的特定事物（特指同类不同物，可数名词单数/不可数名词）。表复数时：用 these、those。

this：进指；that：远指。

The umbrella you bought is chipper than that I bought. 【后面又定语 i bought 修饰，特只用 that 而不是 one】

你买的伞比我买的便宜。（与前面不同的一把伞）

The weather in Lanzhou is cooler than that in SanYa. 【后面有介词短语修饰，所以特只】

兰州的天气比三亚凉爽．（不同的天气，天气不可数）

These pictures are more beautiful than those.

这些照片比那些更漂亮。

2.5 隐性比较点【难点】

(1）Your friend is kind．你的朋友很善良。

Your friend is wise．你的朋友很聪明。

kind 和 wise 无法比，所以只能比程度(much)

→ Your friend is much kind. Your friend is much wise.
→ Your friend is more kind than (he is) (much) wise.

你的朋友善良多过聪明。

(2）His father left him money．他父亲留给他钱。

He can spend money．他可以花钱。

money 和 money 都是名词无法比！但修饰名词的**修饰语（多少）**可以比！

→ His father left him much money. He can spend much money.
→ His father left him more money than he can spend much money.
→ His father left him more money than he can spend. 【省略】
他父亲留给他的钱比他能花的还多。

2.6 最高级

注意！同时满足以下两点：① 范围；② 指定

(1）形容词最高级前通常用 the

例：The Sahara is the biggest desert in the world.

撒哈拉沙漠是世界上最大的沙漠。

(2）形容词 most 前面没有 the，不表示“最”，只表示“非常”；

It is a most important problem.
= It is a very important problem．

这是一个非常重要的问题。

(3）副词最高级前多数不加 the，可用于修饰动词

I shall give a prize to the pupil who reads best.

我将给朗读最好的学生发一份奖品。

(4) 注意！使用最高级要注意将主语包括在比较范围内。

⟡ Tom is the tallest of his three brothers. （×）

⟡ Tom is the tallest of the three brothers.（√）

题目一

If you were to examine the birth certificates of every soccer player in 2006’s World Cup tournament, you would most likely find a noteworthy quirk: elite soccer players are more likely to have been born in the earlier months of the year than in the later months. （还原画线句）

  • noteworthy adj. 值得注目的
  • quirk [kwə:k] n.怪癖，急转，双关语，警句，俏皮话
第一步：还原画线句

（1）Elite soccer players are likely to have been born in the earlier months of the year.

（2）Elite soccer players are likely to have been born in the later months of the year

第二步：找到比较点并简化

句子中没有相关元素可以进行比较，故比较点是隐藏起来的，比的是程度

→ Elite soccer players are much likely to have been born in the earlier months of the year.

​ Elite soccer players are much likely to have been born in the later months of the year.

→ Elite soccer players are more much likely to have been born in the earlier months of the year than elite soccer players are much likely to have been born in the later months of the year.

→ Elite soccer players are more likely to have been born in the earlier months of the year than (elite soccer players) (are (much) likely to have been born) in the later months (of the year).（简化）

→ Elite soccer players are more likely to have been born in the earlier months of the year than in the later months.

译文：如果您查看 2006 年世界杯赛的每位足球运动员的出生证明，极有可能会发现一个奇怪的现象：天才球员不太可能出生在一年中的后半年，而更有可能出生在前半年。

题目二

Critics also argue that commercial genetic testing is only as good as the reference collections to which a sample is compared.

  • commercial [kəˈmɜː®ʃ(ə)l] a.商业的，盈利的
  • reference ['ref(ə)rəns] n.参考，涉及
  • collection [kə’lekʃ(ə)n] n.采集，聚集
第一步：还原画线句

（1）Commercial genetic testing is good.

（2）The reference collections are good.

第二步：找到比较点并简化

寻找比较点，为 good

→ Commercial genetic testing is as good as the reference collections are good.

→ Commercial genetic testing is as good as the reference collections are.

→ Commercial genetic testing is as good as the reference collections.

→ Commercial genetic testing is only as good as the reference collections.

直译：批评者还认为，商业性基因检测仅仅与参照数据库一样好。

意译：批评者还认为，商业性基因检测的好坏仅仅取决于参照数据库的好坏。

意译：批评者还认为，商业性基因检测的准确率仅仅取决于参照数据库的准确率。

题目三

This success, coupled with later research showing that memory itself is not genetically determined, led Ericsson to conclude that the act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one.

  • cognitive [ˈkɒɡnətɪv] adj.认识的，认知的，有认识力的
  • intuitive [ɪnˈtjuːətɪv] adj.直觉的
  • more …than…是否定式比较结构，意为“与其说……，不如说……”
第一步：还原画线句

（1）The act of memorizing is much of a cognitive exercise.

（2）The act of memorizing is much of an intuitive exercise.

第二步：找到比较点并简化

→ The act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than the act of memorizing is much of an intuitive exercise.

→ The act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than (the act of memorizing) (is) (much of) an intuitive one.

→ The act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one.

译文：这一成功，再加上后来的研究（表明记忆本身不是由基因决定的），使得埃里克森得出结论：记忆这种行为与其说是一种直觉行为，倒不如说是一种认知练习所形成的行为
